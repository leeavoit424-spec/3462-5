Our Story

Founded in 1998 in the heart of Cambridge's financial district, GetcinPro began with a simple mission: to provide transparent, data-driven financial advice that empowers businesses to achieve their full potential. What started as a small team of three financial experts has grown into a global consulting firm with clients across 32 countries.

Our approach combines cutting-edge financial technology with decades of hands-on industry experience. We don't just provide reports – we partner with our clients to implement actionable strategies that drive growth, mitigate risk, and optimize financial performance across all business functions.

  • Advanced financial modeling and predictive analytics using AI-powered tools
  • Customized consulting strategies aligned with your business objectives and market conditions
  • Local market expertise with global perspective, ensuring compliance and cultural sensitivity
